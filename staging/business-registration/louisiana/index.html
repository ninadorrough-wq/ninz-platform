How to Register a Business in Louisiana

Staged review draft. Public status: Coming Soon. This page has not been activated.

Evidence reviewed: October 13, 2026Next scheduled review: January 13, 2027

Start with the sequence, then verify what applies.

Structure, activities, employees and location determine the registrations that apply. Complete the applicable actions with the responsible agency.

GeauxBiz is the online business filing portal; confirm account steps and any applicable local requirements. LA-SOS-003

GeauxBiz

Before you begin

  1. Clarify ownership and structure.
  2. Gather the proposed name and address.
  3. Identify activities and operating locations.
  4. Know whether you expect to hire employees.
  5. Keep filing and registration confirmations.
01

Structure

Understand your business structure

The Secretary of State provides entity filing and start-up instructions; select the correct domestic or foreign form. LA-SOS-001

Start a Business
02

Name

Search the proposed business name

The SOS directs a preliminary proposed-name search through its Commercial Database. Search without the corporate ending; the search returns identical or similar records and does not itself reserve the name. LA-SOS-005

Business Filings Frequently Asked Questions
03

State Filing

Complete the applicable Louisiana filing

Domestic LLC Articles of Organization, form #365, cost $125; foreign LLC form #972 costs $185. Filing options and additional charges should be checked at submission. LA-SOS-002

Forms and Fee Schedule
04

Federal EIN

Use the shared IRS EIN guidance

Use current IRS guidance to decide whether your business needs an EIN and which application method is available. An EIN is a federal identifier, separate from state formation, licensing and state tax accounts. IRS-EIN-002

The IRS issues EINs free. If forming a legal entity, complete the state formation before applying, as instructed by the IRS. Use the IRS eligibility and responsible-party instructions directly. IRS-EIN-002

Review IRS EIN guidanceUse the free IRS EIN application
05

State Taxes

Determine Louisiana tax-registration needs

The Department of Revenue directs businesses to determine sales, withholding and other state tax accounts. LA-DOR-001

Business Registration

Register applicable tax accounts through LaTAP according to Revenue instructions. LA-DOR-002

LaTAP
06

Licenses and Permits

Check state and local licenses and permits

Check the responsible agency for occupation- or industry-specific licensing and the locality for permits. Applicable approvals depend on activity and location. LA-SOS-001

Start a Business

Local licenses, zoning and permits depend on the location and activity; verify with the relevant local authority.

07

Employers

Complete applicable employer registrations and coverage

Every employing unit completes the state unemployment application for a liability determination; entity formation alone does not create the UI account. LA-LWC-001

UI Employer Account Information

Unemployment insurance

Use the Workforce Commission employer-registration route to determine UI liability; employer categories have different tests. LA-LWC-002

Employer Registration

Workers compensation

Review the Workers Compensation coverage FAQ for covered employment and specific excluded worker categories. LA-LWC-003

Workers Compensation Coverage FAQ
08

Registered Agent

Confirm registered-agent requirements

LLC filings distinguish registered office and agent; review form #365 instructions before filing. LA-SOS-002

Forms and Fee Schedule
09

Trade Name

Check trade, fictitious or assumed names

The SOS permits a person, corporation, LLC or partnership to apply for a trade name; its fee schedule lists form #309 at $95. The SOS says a DBA is not filed with its office. Determine the appropriate filing and local requirements for the proposed use. LA-SOS-005, LA-SOS-002

Business Filings Frequently Asked Questions
10

Ongoing Requirements

Track ongoing Louisiana requirements

A Louisiana LLC annual report is due on or before the anniversary date of organization. The SOS lists a $35 fee and directs online annual reporting through GeauxBiz or the business record in its Commercial Database. LA-LAW-001, LA-SOS-002, LA-SOS-005

Revised Statutes 12:1308.1

Record the applicable deadlines, agency accounts, responsible person and official sources. Review current instructions before filing.

11

Final Review

Questions to verify before operating

  • Has the correct formation or foreign-authority filing been completed?
  • Which tax accounts, professional or activity licenses and local permits apply?
  • Are employer registration, reporting and coverage ready?
  • Which entity-specific reports and renewals need calendar reminders?

Portal review note: GeauxBiz and LaTAP require interactive account review; the SOS name search uses a challenge. Confirm the applicable forms and filing path without relying on URL availability alone.

Activation blockers for this review draft
