How to Register a Business in Kansas

Staged review draft. Public status: Coming Soon. This page has not been activated.

Evidence reviewed: October 4, 2026Next scheduled review: January 2, 2027

Start with the sequence, then verify what applies.

Structure, activities, employees and location determine the registrations that apply. Complete the applicable actions with the responsible agency.

Kansas Business One Stop links state formation, taxes, licenses and employer resources. KS-PORTAL-001

Business Center One Stop

Before you begin

  1. Clarify ownership and structure.
  2. Gather the proposed name and address.
  3. Identify activities and operating locations.
  4. Know whether you expect to hire employees.
  5. Keep filing and registration confirmations.
01

Structure

Understand your business structure

Kansas SOS registers corporations, LLCs, LLPs, LPs and other covered filing entities; applicable foreign entities must register as well. KS-SOS-001

Register a Business
02

Name

Search the proposed business name

Use the official Name Availability tool and business search before submitting the entity filing. Confirm the chosen name under current SOS instructions. KS-SOS-002

Business Name Availability
03

State Filing

Complete the applicable Kansas filing

Sole proprietors do not register with Kansas SOS. General partnerships are not required to register, but may file a Statement of Partnership Authority. Select the correct domestic or foreign filing for entities required to register. KS-SOS-001

Register a Business
04

Federal EIN

Use the shared IRS EIN guidance

Use current IRS guidance to decide whether your business needs an EIN and which application method is available. An EIN is a federal identifier, separate from state formation, licensing and state tax accounts. IRS-EIN-002

The IRS issues EINs free. If forming a legal entity, complete the state formation before applying, as instructed by the IRS. Use the IRS eligibility and responsible-party instructions directly. IRS-EIN-002

Review IRS EIN guidanceUse the free IRS EIN application

KDOR instructs applicants who intend to obtain an EIN to get it before completing the Kansas Business Tax Application. This state sequencing instruction does not decide federal EIN eligibility. KS-DOR-002

05

State Taxes

Determine Kansas tax-registration needs

KDOR administers state business tax registration separately from SOS formation and KDOL unemployment registration. KS-DOR-001

Business Tax Registration

Use KDOR's Customer Service Center or CR-16 application for applicable sales/use, withholding and other business-tax accounts. Requirements depend on activity; registration is not a universal professional license. KS-DOR-002

KS-1216 Business Tax Application
06

Licenses and Permits

Check state and local licenses and permits

Check state licensing boards for your activity and the city or county for local business licenses and permits. Formation and tax registration do not establish that all operating approvals are complete. KS-PORTAL-002

Research required: An explicit statewide general-business-license conclusion has not been verified from current official evidence. Research is required before activation. KS-PORTAL-002

Obtain Business Licenses and Permits

Ask each city or county where the business operates about business licensing, zoning, occupancy, home-business and activity-specific permits. Check the responsible state licensing agency as well.

07

Employers

Complete applicable employer registrations and coverage

Research required: Use KDOL's employer Status Report to obtain a liability determination and review its employer registration instructions. KDOL also provides a new-hire reporting form. KS-DOL-002

Register for Kansas wage withholding with KDOR when the applicable payments require it. This is a separate obligation from unemployment insurance. KS-DOR-002

Employer Forms

Unemployment insurance

Under K.S.A. 44-703(h), the general employer test includes $1,500 or more wages in any quarter of the current or preceding year, or at least one worker for part of a day in each of 20 different weeks in either year. Domestic and agricultural employment use separate tests; voluntary, successor and federal-conformity provisions can also establish liability. KS-LAW-001

Separate tests include domestic service with $1,000 or more cash remuneration in a quarter; agricultural labor with $20,000 or more cash remuneration in a quarter or at least 10 workers in each of 20 different weeks; and covered 501(c)(3) employers with four or more workers in each of 20 different weeks. These tests use the current or preceding year. Governmental employment and statutory exclusions require separate review; this list is not exhaustive. KS-LAW-001

Research required: Verify the current KDOL Status Report, account-registration sequence and category instructions before activation. Statutory liability verification does not establish that the blocked agency registration path works. KS-DOL-001, KS-DOL-002

Unemployment Tax

Workers compensation

Non-agricultural employers generally need coverage when gross annual payroll exceeds $20,000. Include wages paid in and outside Kansas in the calculation; corporate payroll includes family wages. KS-DOL-003

Certain agriculture, qualifying real-estate agents and other statutory exclusions apply. Proprietors, partners and LLC members differ from ordinary employees. Elections may allow coverage or an eligible owner's exclusion; they do not remove coverage duties for other employees. KS-DOL-003

Workers Compensation Division
08

Registered Agent

Confirm registered-agent requirements

Review Kansas resident-agent eligibility and registered-office instructions for the entity. Use a qualified Kansas agent and keep the official address current. KS-PORTAL-003

Plan to Register a Business
09

Trade Name

Check trade, fictitious or assumed names

The SOS name-reservation application reserves a future entity name; it does not register an assumed, fictitious, trade or DBA name. KS-SOS-001

Research required: Verify any activity-specific or local assumed-name requirements before asserting that a DBA filing is unnecessary. No broader absence-of-filing conclusion has been verified. KS-PORTAL-002

Register a Business
10

Ongoing Requirements

Track ongoing Kansas requirements

Information reports are filed every two years, keyed to the entity's odd/even formation year. For-profit businesses are due April 15 and not-for-profit businesses June 15 in the applicable report year. KS-SOS-003

Keep resident-agent and registered-office details current; tax, employer and license filings remain separate. KS-SOS-003

Information Reports

Record the applicable deadlines, agency accounts, responsible person and official sources. Review current instructions before filing.

11

Final Review

Questions to verify before operating

  • Has the correct formation or foreign-authority filing been completed?
  • Which tax accounts, professional or activity licenses and local permits apply?
  • Are employer registration, reporting and coverage ready?
  • Which entity-specific reports and renewals need calendar reminders?

Activation blockers for this review draft
