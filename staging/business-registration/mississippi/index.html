How to Register a Business in Mississippi

Staged review draft. Public status: Coming Soon. This page has not been activated.

Evidence reviewed: October 13, 2026Next scheduled review: January 13, 2027

Start with the sequence, then verify what applies.

Structure, activities, employees and location determine the registrations that apply. Complete the applicable actions with the responsible agency.

The business filing portal is the online SOS route; confirm the form and account steps there. MS-SOS-002

Business Filing Portal

Before you begin

  1. Clarify ownership and structure.
  2. Gather the proposed name and address.
  3. Identify activities and operating locations.
  4. Know whether you expect to hire employees.
  5. Keep filing and registration confirmations.

Understand your business structure

03

State Filing

Complete the applicable Mississippi filing

The SOS services-and-fees schedule lists a $50 Certificate of Formation for a Mississippi LLC and a $250 Application for Registration of a Foreign LLC. Select the correct entity filing in the SOS portal. MS-SOS-003

Services and Fees (revised October 2024)
04

Federal EIN

Use the shared IRS EIN guidance

Use current IRS guidance to decide whether your business needs an EIN and which application method is available. An EIN is a federal identifier, separate from state formation, licensing and state tax accounts. IRS-EIN-002

The IRS issues EINs free. If forming a legal entity, complete the state formation before applying, as instructed by the IRS. Use the IRS eligibility and responsible-party instructions directly. IRS-EIN-002

Review IRS EIN guidanceUse the free IRS EIN application

State Taxes

Determine Mississippi tax-registration needs

The Department of Revenue administers applicable sales, use and other business tax accounts. MS-DOR-001

Register for Taxes

Use Taxpayer Access Point for applicable tax registration according to DOR instructions. MS-DOR-002

Workers compensation

08

Registered Agent

Confirm registered-agent requirements

A noncommercial registered agent must have a physical Mississippi address and consent to serve; a commercial registered agent follows a separate registration process. Confirm the selected agent in the filing portal. MS-SOS-005

Registered Agents
09

Trade Name

Check trade, fictitious or assumed names

Research required: The SOS fee schedule lists Fictitious Business Name Registration at $25. Confirm whether the proposed name and entity require or elect this filing before relying on it. MS-SOS-003

Services and Fees (revised October 2024)
10

Ongoing Requirements

Track ongoing Mississippi requirements

For-profit corporation and LLC annual reports are due April 15, nonprofit reports May 15; reports are filed online. The SOS fee schedule lists no fee for a Mississippi LLC report and $250 for a foreign LLC report. MS-SOS-004, MS-SOS-003

Annual Reports

Record the applicable deadlines, agency accounts, responsible person and official sources. Review current instructions before filing.

11

Final Review

Questions to verify before operating

  • Has the correct formation or foreign-authority filing been completed?
  • Which tax accounts, professional or activity licenses and local permits apply?
  • Are employer registration, reporting and coverage ready?
  • Which entity-specific reports and renewals need calendar reminders?

Portal review note: The SOS business filing, TAP and MDES account routes require interactive review. The SOS fee schedule establishes the listed fees; confirm the applicable form and unresolved name or coverage rules.

Activation blockers for this review draft

  • Confirm workers compensation threshold and exclusions using current Commission or statutory guidance; the available facts PDF is dated 2013.
  • Confirm current fictitious-name applicability; the $25 fee is verified.
  • Verify activity-specific licensing for the proposed business.
  • Confirm any account-only SOS, TAP or MDES form essential to the selected filing.
  • Complete a genuine mobile-width visual and keyboard review.
  • Complete desktop/mobile browser, keyboard and visual accessibility review before activation.
