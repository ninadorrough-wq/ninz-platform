Workers compensation

Mississippi generally requires workers compensation coverage for qualifying private employers with five or more regularly employed workers, subject to statutory exclusions and other applicable provisions. Section 71-3-5 identifies excluded categories, voluntary coverage, and a written election by qualifying sole proprietors, partners and corporate employees owning at least 15 percent of stock to be exempt from coverage and the five-worker count. Whether a particular worker counts depends on the law and employment relationship; consult the Mississippi Workers Compensation Commission and, when necessary, a qualified Mississippi attorney for the proposed operation. MS-LAW-001, MS-COURT-001

Confirm registered-agent requirements

A noncommercial registered agent must have a physical Mississippi address and consent to serve; a commercial registered agent follows a separate registration process. Confirm the selected agent in the filing portal. MS-SOS-005

10

Ongoing Requirements

Track ongoing Mississippi requirements

For-profit corporation and LLC annual reports are due April 15, nonprofit reports May 15; reports are filed online. The SOS fee schedule lists no fee for a Mississippi LLC report and $250 for a foreign LLC report. MS-SOS-004, MS-SOS-003

Annual Reports

Record the applicable deadlines, agency accounts, responsible person and official sources. Review current instructions before filing.
