How to Register a Business in New Mexico

Staged review draft. Public status: Coming Soon. This page has not been activated.

Evidence reviewed: October 13, 2026Next scheduled review: January 13, 2027

Start with the sequence, then verify what applies.

Structure, activities, employees and location determine the registrations that apply. Complete the applicable actions with the responsible agency.

In SOS Enterprise, sign in, choose Forms, then Business and the entity type, and select File Online. Upload the required registered-agent acceptance PDF; the form description displays its filing fee and the My Business Work Queue tracks review. NM-SOS-005

Business Online Filing Help (December 2024)

Before you begin

  1. Clarify ownership and structure.
  2. Gather the proposed name and address.
  3. Identify activities and operating locations.
  4. Know whether you expect to hire employees.
  5. Keep filing and registration confirmations.

03

State Filing

Complete the applicable New Mexico filing

Research required: A domestic LLC files Articles of Organization and a foreign LLC files for registration before transacting business. The Enterprise form index lists the domestic and foreign LLC forms; confirm each current fee in its form description before filing. NM-RULE-001, NM-SOS-003, NM-SOS-005

04

Federal EIN

Use the shared IRS EIN guidance

Use current IRS guidance to decide whether your business needs an EIN and which application method is available. An EIN is a federal identifier, separate from state formation, licensing and state tax accounts. IRS-EIN-002

The IRS issues EINs free. If forming a legal entity, complete the state formation before applying, as instructed by the IRS. Use the IRS eligibility and responsible-party instructions directly. IRS-EIN-002

State Taxes

Determine New Mexico tax-registration needs

Businesses meeting New Mexico registration criteria must obtain a Business Tax Identification Number; check tax types and applicability. NM-TRD-001

Who Must Register a Business?

Use the Taxation and Revenue online services for applicable business tax registration; the Business Tax ID application is free. NM-TRD-002

Online Services
06

Licenses and Permits

Check state and local licenses and permits

Research required: Check professional, activity and local permits; statewide general-license applicability has not been conclusively verified. NM-SOS-001

Business Services

Local licenses, zoning and permits depend on the location and activity; verify with the relevant local authority.

07

Employers

Complete applicable employer registrations and coverage

Determine unemployment tax liability and registration with the Department of Workforce Solutions. NM-DWS-001

UI Tax Information

Unemployment insurance

New employers subject to UI tax register through the DWS Unemployment Insurance Tax Self-Service System. The official employer registration page creates an Employer Account Number; determine liability and any special category using agency instructions. NM-DWS-002

Employer UI Tax Registration

Workers compensation

Coverage generally applies at three or more workers and to licensed construction activities regardless of headcount. The WCA counts part-time, seasonal and paid family workers; executive owners may elect exclusion from their own coverage but still count toward the three-worker threshold. Confirm the entity and activity-specific exceptions. NM-WCA-002

Frequently Asked Questions
08

Registered Agent

Confirm registered-agent requirements

Domestic and foreign LLCs continuously maintain a New Mexico registered agent and office; upload the agent Statement of Acceptance PDF with the Enterprise application. NM-RULE-001, NM-SOS-005

09

Trade Name

Check trade, fictitious or assumed names

Research required: The SOS resources page links a fictitious-name resolution for a corporation whose true name was rejected; that form does not establish an LLC trade-name filing. Confirm the rule and authority for the proposed LLC name. NM-SOS-006

10

Ongoing Requirements

Track ongoing New Mexico requirements

Research required: Check entity-specific ongoing reports and deadlines in the current online forms; do not assume a universal LLC annual report. NM-SOS-003

Business Forms

Record the applicable deadlines, agency accounts, responsible person and official sources. Review current instructions before filing.

11

Final Review

Questions to verify before operating

  • Has the correct formation or foreign-authority filing been completed?
  • Which tax accounts, professional or activity licenses and local permits apply?
  • Are employer registration, reporting and coverage ready?
  • Which entity-specific reports and renewals need calendar reminders?

Portal review note: The SOS filing guide identifies the Enterprise Forms > Business > entity type path and registered-agent acceptance upload. Confirm current LLC fees in the form descriptions and any report or trade-name filing applicable to the selected entity; the linked fictitious-name resolution is corporation-specific.

Activation blockers for this review draft
