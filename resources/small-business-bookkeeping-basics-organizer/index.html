Working page 1 of 6

Bookkeeping setup

Create a repeatable process for collecting records. Use account nicknames only.

Keep business and personal activity distinguishable. If they have been mixed, gather the records and ask a qualified professional how to document and correct the situation. Do not assume that a separate account alone completes your bookkeeping.

Do not enter account numbers, tax identification numbers, passwords, card details, or confidential customer information in this organizer. Store the completed file securely.

Working page 3 of 6

Income and expense categories

Choose consistent labels that fit your activity. Confirm classification and tax treatment with a professional.

Income examples: product sales, service fees, subscriptions. Track refunds and discounts clearly. Transfers, owner contributions, and loan proceeds need separate treatment from sales; ask how they should be recorded.

Expense examples: supplies, software, advertising, rent, professional services, payment fees. A bookkeeping label does not establish deductibility. Ask about inventory, equipment, mixed-use purchases, and owner withdrawals.
