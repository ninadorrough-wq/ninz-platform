Responsible AI for Business Checklist

A practical checklist for using AI with human review, privacy awareness, and care before publishing, sending, or relying on outputs.

Download Fillable PDFBack to Free Resources

Resource Type

Web + Fillable PDF

Web checklist plus fillable PDF for public use.

Checklist

Before using AI

  • Is the task appropriate for AI?
  • Does it involve sensitive information?
  • Can identifying information be removed?
  • Does the task require licensed professional judgment?
  • Could an error cause harm or confusion?

Checklist

While using AI

  • Give appropriate context.
  • Avoid unnecessary confidential information.
  • Understand tool settings where relevant.
  • Ask for sources when factual support matters.
  • Keep the business owner or team member in control.

Checklist

Before using output

  • Review accuracy.
  • Verify material claims.
  • Check tone and context.
  • Confirm business policies.
  • Check legal, financial, tax, medical, or professional issues where appropriate.
  • Maintain human oversight.

Checklist

Before publishing or sending

  • Is it accurate?
  • Is it appropriate?
  • Does it disclose anything private?
  • Would I stand behind this as the business owner?
