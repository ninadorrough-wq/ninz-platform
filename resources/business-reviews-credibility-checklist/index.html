NINZ Organizing Framework

The CLEAR Credibility Check

Use five customer-facing areas to organize your review and identify what needs attention.

CConsistent facts
LLegible offer
EEvidence with context
AAccessible next steps
RResponsive review handling
What this framework is not

The CLEAR Credibility Check is a NINZ organizing framework. It is not a score, certification, ranking model, prediction model, or legal compliance determination.

What It Helps You Do

See the business as a customer does.

Review the public information, profiles, website details, customer next steps, reviews, testimonials, and response practices that may shape a customer's understanding of your business.

Use Clear, Needs Attention, or Not Applicable for each checklist item. Then identify the three most important credibility gaps and choose one correction to complete this week.
