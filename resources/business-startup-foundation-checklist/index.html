Business Startup & Foundation Checklist

A practical checklist to help new and early-stage business owners organize foundational business considerations before moving forward.

Download Printable PDFBack to Free Resources

Resource Type

Web + Printable PDF

Web checklist first, then printable PDF after review.

Checklist

What this helps you do

Use this checklist to organize the early decisions, records, and questions that often come up when starting or strengthening a small business.

  • Clarify your business idea and intended customer.
  • Organize business identity information.
  • Identify registrations, licenses, permits, and tax items that may apply.
  • Prepare contact, banking, and recordkeeping foundations.
  • Decide where professional help may be needed.

Checklist

Who it is for

This resource is for business owners who want a plain-language starting point. It is especially useful if you are still organizing your business name, structure, records, contact information, compliance reminders, or next steps.

Checklist

How to use it

  1. Read each section before taking action.
  2. Mark items that apply to your business.
  3. Write down questions that need official verification.
  4. Use the related FAQs to understand topics before making decisions.
  5. Review the checklist periodically as your business changes.

This is an educational checklist, not a universal legal sequence. Some items may not apply to your business.

Checklist

Resource preview

Business Idea

  • Define your product or service.
  • Identify your intended customer.
  • Complete basic market research.
  • Consider whether a business plan would help.

Business Identity

  • Research possible business names.
  • Consider business structure options.
  • Identify registration steps that may apply.
  • Document DBA or trade-name questions if relevant.

Business Identifiers

  • Determine whether you need an EIN.
  • Identify state or local registrations that may apply.
  • Store business identification details in an organized place.

Business Contact Foundation

  • Consider what address is appropriate for each business purpose.
  • Consider whether a business phone number would help.
  • Create or organize a business email address.
  • Review domain and online presence needs.

Financial Organization

  • Determine whether a business bank account is appropriate.
  • Identify payment methods you may need.
  • Set up a recordkeeping or bookkeeping process.
  • Store financial records in an organized way.

Compliance

  • Identify licenses or permits that may apply.
  • Identify tax registrations that may apply.
  • Determine whether registered-agent requirements apply.
  • Track ongoing filing, renewal, or reporting responsibilities.

Next Steps

  • Create a compliance calendar.
  • Store important documents.
  • Identify professional help needed.
  • Review your business foundation periodically.

Continue Your Journey

What to explore next.

After completing this checklist, organize your key business details in one place and create a calendar for verified renewal dates, filing dates, and important reminders.
