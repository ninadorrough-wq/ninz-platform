Confidentiality Policy

Information on this website is for general business purposes and does not constitute legal, financial, or professional advice.

Last Updated: August 21, 2026

Client Information

NINZ respects client business information. Client-provided materials, systems, login details, business processes, customer information, and visibility review information are treated as confidential.

Use of Information

NINZ uses client information only as needed to provide requested services, communicate with the client, plan projects, configure systems, conduct authorized monitoring and periodic visibility reviews, prepare AI visibility observations and practical recommendations, and support business solutions.

Third-Party Platforms

NINZ may use third-party platforms to deliver services, including landing page, form, messaging, automation, hosting, analytics, monitoring, payment, and communication tools. These platforms may process information according to their own terms and policies.

Sensitive Information

Clients should avoid submitting sensitive medical, legal, financial, or protected information unless specifically requested and authorized. NINZ does not provide HIPAA-regulated medical services through the website, chatbot, or concierge unless a separate written agreement is in place.

Security Limits

NINZ takes reasonable steps to protect information, but no digital system can be guaranteed completely secure. Clients are responsible for maintaining secure passwords and access controls for their own systems.
