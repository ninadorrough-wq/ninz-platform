# NINZ Navigator — First Five-State Cohort Research Record

Working branch: authority-platform-expansion-2026-10-03
Research date: 2026-10-03
Publication state: UNPUBLISHED / QA REQUIRED

This record tracks official-source research for Texas, Kansas, Arkansas, Missouri, and Colorado before activation in the NINZ Business Registration Navigator. Oklahoma remains the published baseline.

## Shared federal module
Federal EIN guidance must point to the IRS Employer Identification Number guidance. State records should reference, not duplicate, federal rules.

## Texas
- Filing authority: Texas Secretary of State.
- Formation/name rules: entity names for filing entities must comply with Texas Business Organizations Code name-availability rules; SOS provides formation forms and filing pathways.
- Taxes: Texas Comptroller. A sales tax permit is required when a business is engaged in Texas and sells/leases/rents taxable goods, provides taxable services, or meets stated use-tax conditions.
- Licensing: Texas does not use one statewide general business license; activity/profession and local requirements must be checked.
- Unemployment/employer: Texas Workforce Commission employer/unemployment tax guidance must be linked in the final state record.
- Workers' compensation: most private employers may choose whether to carry coverage. Non-subscribers have state notice/reporting duties. Governmental entities must carry coverage.
- Local caveat required.
- Ongoing reporting/franchise-tax obligations must be verified with the Comptroller before activation.

## Kansas
- Filing authority: Kansas Secretary of State for filing entities.
- Entity distinction: not every business structure files with the Secretary of State; sole proprietorship/general partnership treatment must be stated carefully.
- Taxes: Kansas Department of Revenue Customer Service Center / Business Tax Registration. KDOR notes businesses may also need Secretary of State and Department of Labor registrations.
- EIN sequencing: KDOR states that if a business intends to apply for a federal EIN, it should obtain it before completing the Kansas Business Tax Application.
- Licensing: state/activity and local requirements must be checked; do not imply one universal license.
- Unemployment/employer: Kansas Department of Labor.
- Workers' compensation: final public copy must preserve statutory threshold/exceptions rather than reduce the rule to a universal mandate.
- Ongoing reporting: Secretary of State reporting/maintenance path must be verified before activation.

## Arkansas
- Filing authority: Arkansas Secretary of State.
- State startup/tax/licensing/employer records will use Arkansas official state portals and responsible agencies.
- Workers' compensation and unemployment rules require agency-specific verification before activation.
- Local licensing/permit caveat required.
- Do not activate until all material links and thresholds are captured in the structured state record.

## Missouri
- Filing authority: Missouri Secretary of State.
- Official startup sequence separates entity formation, tax registration, unemployment/employer obligations, workers' compensation, and local licensing.
- State tax path: Missouri Department of Revenue.
- Unemployment/employer path: Missouri labor/workforce authority.
- Workers' compensation rule and exceptions must be verified from the responsible Missouri authority.
- Local city/county licensing caveat required.
- Ongoing entity reporting requirements must be verified by entity type before activation.

## Colorado
- Filing authority: Colorado Secretary of State.
- State business filing and periodic-report pathways must be captured separately from tax/licensing.
- Tax: Colorado Department of Revenue.
- Licensing: state/professional/activity requirements plus local requirements; do not imply one universal business license.
- Unemployment/employer: Colorado Department of Labor and Employment.
- Workers' compensation: employers with one or more employees generally must carry coverage, subject to official exceptions/classifications.
- Ongoing reporting: periodic report requirements/path must be verified before activation.
