# NINZ Navigator Cohort 01 — Verified Requirements Addendum

Status: NON-PRODUCTION / ACTIVATION QA REQUIRED
Verified: 2026-10-04

This addendum records responsible-agency findings that have passed an official-source check. It does not activate any state guide.

## Texas
- No statewide general business license; activity-specific, professional, and local requirements may apply.
- Filing entities use the Texas Secretary of State formation and name framework.
- Texas franchise tax applies to taxable entities formed or organized in Texas or doing business in Texas; current reporting details belong to the Comptroller.
- Most private employers may choose whether to carry workers compensation coverage. Governmental entities must carry coverage. Non-subscribing private employers have notice/reporting duties.
Authorities: Texas Secretary of State; Texas Comptroller of Public Accounts; Office of the Governor Business Permit Office; Texas Department of Insurance.

## Kansas
- Corporations, LLCs, LLPs, LPs, and other covered filing entities register with the Kansas Secretary of State. General partnerships are not required to register; sole proprietors do not register with the Secretary of State.
- Kansas Department of Revenue instructs applicants who intend to obtain a federal EIN to obtain it before completing the Kansas Business Tax Application.
- Workers compensation generally applies to non-agricultural employers with gross annual payroll over $20,000, subject to statutory exclusions and elections.
Authorities: Kansas Secretary of State; Kansas Department of Revenue; Kansas Department of Labor.

## Arkansas
- Corporations, LLCs, and specified entity types file with the Arkansas Secretary of State; applicable foreign entities may also need registration.
- Many state licenses are activity-specific through boards or commissions; cities may impose local privilege or business licensing.
- Corporations and LLCs registered in Arkansas are subject to the Secretary of State annual franchise-tax framework; entity-specific current obligations must be checked before activation.
- Arkansas Division of Workforce Services maintains employer unemployment-insurance account services.
Remaining gate: responsible-agency verification of current tax-registration and workers-compensation rules.

## Missouri
- The Secretary of State Corporations Unit handles creation and maintenance filings for domestic and applicable out-of-state entities.
- Department of Revenue registration depends on tax activity, including sales/use, withholding, and corporate income tax where applicable; online new-business registration can also cover unemployment tax.
- Workers compensation generally applies at five or more employees, or one or more employees for construction employers, subject to statutory exemptions/elections.
- Ongoing filing requirements are entity-specific and must not be presented as one identical annual filing for every entity.
Authorities: Missouri Secretary of State; Missouri Department of Revenue; Missouri Department of Labor and Industrial Relations.

## Colorado
- Employers paying wages to at least one Colorado employee can use state employer-registration systems for unemployment insurance, subject to category-specific instructions.
- Employers generally must maintain workers compensation coverage for one or more full- or part-time employees, with limited exceptions.
- Reporting entities use periodic reports to maintain entity information and good standing.
Remaining gate: responsible-agency verification of current formation/name, tax-registration, licensing/local-requirement, and periodic-report destinations before activation.
