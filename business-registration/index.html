What this Navigator does

NINZ organizes official starting points into a practical sequence so you can understand which government offices may be involved. It does not reproduce government applications, choose a business structure for you, or determine which requirements apply to your specific situation.

NINZ is not a government agency and is not affiliated with any state or federal agency. This Navigator provides general educational information, not legal, tax, accounting, employment, licensing, financial, or other professional advice.
