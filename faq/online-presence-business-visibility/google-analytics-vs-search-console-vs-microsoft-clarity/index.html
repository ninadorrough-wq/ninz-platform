What’s the Difference Between Google Analytics, Google Search Console, and Microsoft Clarity?

Google Search Console reports how a site interacts with Google Search, including search performance and indexing information. Google Analytics reports configured website or app interactions and acquisition data. Microsoft Clarity provides behavioral views such as recordings and heatmaps. They complement one another but do not measure the same thing.
