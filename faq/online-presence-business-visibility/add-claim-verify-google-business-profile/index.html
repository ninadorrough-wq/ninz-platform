How Do I Add, Claim, and Verify My Google Business Profile?

If your business does not already have a Google Business Profile, you can add one at no charge. If a profile already exists but is unverified, you may be able to claim it instead. After adding or claiming the profile, Google requires verification before you have full control over how the business information appears. Available verification methods can vary by business.
