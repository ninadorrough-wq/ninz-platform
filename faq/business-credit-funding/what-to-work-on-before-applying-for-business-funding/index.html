What Should I Work on Before Applying for Business Funding?

Before applying, know why funding is needed, how much is needed, and how repayment may work. Organize business records, banking, financial documents, credit awareness, use-of-funds details, and lender comparison criteria. Apply intentionally rather than reacting to every funding advertisement.
