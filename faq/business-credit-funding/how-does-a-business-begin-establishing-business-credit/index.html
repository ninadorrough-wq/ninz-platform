How Does a Business Begin Establishing Business Credit?

A business begins establishing business credit by creating a clear business foundation, maintaining accurate business identity information, using legitimate credit relationships when appropriate, understanding whether accounts report, paying obligations on time, monitoring business credit information, and correcting inaccuracies through proper processes.
